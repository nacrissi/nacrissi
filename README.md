## Jhinna Salinas

**Systems-thinking operations & automation for small remote teams.**
I map the tension, then build the Apps Script and AI workflows that give teams their week back.

Eleven years as a massage therapist taught me to read systems: where the tension is, what it's
compensating for, how the parts connect. Workflows break the same way bodies do. So I don't
automate a broken process. I map the tension first, find where the system actually breaks, then
build the fix. That throughline runs from bodies, to operations, to AI.

- Process design that survives contact with reality

### Proof
Three small, working rebuilds of real automations, written from scratch with invented data. Each runs in two minutes and shows its work.
- [weekly-report-autopilot](https://github.com/nacrissi/weekly-report-autopilot): a weekly leadership deck from six sources that refuses to build when the data is wrong
- [record-linkage-toolkit](https://github.com/nacrissi/record-linkage-toolkit): matches messy store names to a master list, sends the unsure ones to a person, and learns from their answers
- [roster-reconciler](https://github.com/nacrissi/roster-reconciler): keeps one roster in sync across four systems, with 2FA and confirmation gates and a run that's safe to repeat
